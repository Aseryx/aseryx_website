## How it works

**Claim:** One path. From a connected database to access you control for a set time.

**01 Choose** · Start with your connected database.  
Pick tables and fields from the connected database.

**02 Package** · Commit the chosen dataset.  
Encrypted on your machine for appraisal and access.

**03 Appraise** · Prove the dataset's quality.  
Quality certificate before you grant access.

**04 Term** · Grant access to the dataset.  
Choose who and for how long. Access ends when the time ends.
