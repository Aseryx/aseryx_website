## How it works

**Claim:** Scan for tables and fields that belong together. Lock them as a dataset and check if they are useful. The database stays where it is. When you are ready, you give access for a time you set.

**01 Choose**  
Start with your live database.  
Pick a coherent dataset: tables, joins, and fields from what is already running. Not the whole database.

**02 Lock**  
Lock that as a dataset.  
The database stays where it is. You lock a working set from what you already run, not an upload of the whole thing.

**03 Check**  
Verify the dataset is useful.  
The check says whether that dataset has utility: structure, completeness, distinctiveness, balance. It is not the sale.

**04 Term**  
Keep the verified dataset on your side.  
You get a real dataset from what you already run, plus a clear answer about quality. When you open access, you set the time.

## FAQ (two only)

**What does Aseryx give you?**  
A way to scan your live database, lock a dataset, and check if it is useful. When it passes, you have a data asset still on your side. You give access for a time you set. The database stays where it is.

**Where does Aseryx run?**  
Where your database already is. When you lock, we store an encrypted dataset of what you picked. Some setups keep everything on your side; others move samples through our cloud. We say which path you are on before you commit.
