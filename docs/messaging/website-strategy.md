# Public website strategy

**Status.** Working strategy for `aseryx.xyz`. Guides Home and related public surfaces.  
**Related.** Bootstrapping V3.2 (`aseryx_application/Bootstrapping v3.2.docx.md`) is the 90-day commercial door. `docs/messaging/website-copy.md` is the current locked copy sheet for PR #22. This document governs how those relate.  
**Date.** 9 September 2026.

---

## Core split

Give the site one durable job. Keep the 90-day bootstrap off the homepage hero.

| Layer | Audience | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public website | Broad, skeptical, searchable | Explain what Aseryx is, honestly |
| Sales / first meeting | Named owner with a real buyer | Close the 90-day door |
| Product / docs | Users and builders | Exact surfaces, constraints, how it works |

If the homepage says “owners with a buyer,” sales copy is running as brand. That narrows the company before the experiment has proved itself.

**Operating rule.** Public site answers: what is Aseryx, and can I trust the claims? Sales answers: is this a deal we should run this quarter?

---

## What the website optimizes for

1. **Clarity of the object**  
   Proprietary data → defined cut → frozen copy → paid, time-bounded access.  
   Not Score. Not marketplace. Not “know what your data is worth.”

2. **Trust through honesty**  
   Custody by path. Quality optional. Listing is an explicit choice. Grant binds a dataset name today.  
   Public trust comes from not overclaiming.

3. **Optionality of GTM**  
   Site stays true if the first win is a named-pair license or if the door widens later.  
   Bootstrap language can change. Product truth should not thrash.

4. **A single clear CTA**  
   “Talk to us” / “Open a license” is fine. Qualify hard in the conversation, not in the H1.

## Messaging architecture

### Hero (universal)

License proprietary data without dumping the corpus.  
Build a defined cut, freeze it, disclose custody, grant a fixed term.

Keep the bootstrap filter (“owners with a buyer”) out of the H1 and primary meta title.

### Proof band (honest)

Custody said by step · Quality is optional · Term ends

### How it works (spine)

Pick → Lock → Open  

Maps to Workspace → Vault → Access. Appraisal is an optional quality packet on the locked copy, not the product people came to buy. Score is the number inside Appraisal, not a SKU and not a price.

### Who it’s for (soft)

Rights holders with proprietary data they can bound and license.  
“Strongest path is a real counterparty” belongs here as guidance, not as a gate that makes cold traffic bounce.

### Not for (honest, short)

- Score-hunting with nothing to sell  
- Brokerage that finds demand  
- Claims the stack cannot keep (never leaves, fitness proven, listing optional)

### Close

Ready when you have a defined use to license.  
Qualify “buyer named” on the call.

## Website vs bootstrap pitch

| Surface | Framing |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Durable product truth. Universal enough not to look temporary. Disciplined enough to survive diligence. |
| Bootstrap / first meeting | Named owner, named paying buyer, paid term, no Exchange tour. Conversion experiment against email + shared folder + counsel. |

The bootstrap memo is a **90-day door**, not a permanent company definition. Selection is not license-only forever. GTM is not locked. Product language may follow the memo’s honesty rules without replacing the public site with the sales filter.

### How Bootstrapping V3.2 was misused

Bootstrapping V3.2 was treated as a **public-site brief** when it is a **commercial-door memo**.

Correct uses of the memo:

- Qualify deals and first meetings (named owner, named buyer, paid term)
- Ban claims the stack cannot keep (never leaves, Score as product, optional listing, hash-bound grant before it ships)
- Keep Appraisal as a quality packet, not what the buyer came to buy
- Sequence Exchange off the default tour

Incorrect use (what happened on the website copy path / PR #22):

- Locking Home H1, title, and SEO to “Data licensing for owners with a buyer”
- Making “buyer first” the brand gate instead of a sales filter
- Collapsing a 90-day conversion experiment into the permanent public sentence for Aseryx

The memo itself says it is research and development, not locked GTM, and that product language follows it without replacing shipped code. Honesty rules travel to the site. The ICP door does not become the homepage.

### First-meeting talk track (sales, not Home H1)

1. Who wants it, and for what?  
2. Can you bound that ask to an exact cut (tables, columns, SQL)?  
3. We build that cut, freeze it, disclose custody by path, then run pay-and-grant.  
4. We are not selling a Score.  
5. We are not touring Exchange as the win.

### Buyer line (counterparty, not the ICP)

Time-bounded grant to a named vaulted dataset, custody disclosed, optional quality packet. Not an open-ended dump of the whole corpus, and not a hash-bound exact cut until that ships.

## Say / do not say

| Say | Do not say |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace selects the cut; Vault freezes it | Workspace selects and freezes; Appraisal selects the cut |
| Appraisal is a quality packet on vaulted bytes | Score as the product, hero, or SKU |
| License / grant on a dataset name | Listing or a scoreboard as the win |
| Runtime scan keeps cells on this machine; Cloud may transit samples; Vault CID is public | Data never leaves; zero custody transfer; zero custody risk |
| Prove currently lists after Appraisal | Listing is optional; we do not list unless you choose |
| Grant binds a dataset name today | Exact-cut / freeze-hash delivery (until that ships) |
| Human quote sets the price | Score or fitness is the price |
| Queries attest a requester predicate | Workspace SQL is the Query proof; fitness already proven |

### Claims the stack cannot keep (public ban)

- Data never leaves / nothing is transmitted / zero custody transfer  
- AI builders see a verified Score / know what your data is worth  
- Appraisal selects a cut for the buyer’s stated use  
- The licensed object is the exact cut hash the buyer asked for  
- Fitness for your use is already proven by the product  
- HIPAA-ready because data never moves  
- Come name a use for your own agents this quarter (not the public door)

## What not to do

- Do not lock the whole public brand to the 90-day ICP filter.  
- Do not keep `/buyers`, `/datasets`, or legal copy teaching “zero custody” / “no raw data transmitted” / “marketplace” while Home is clean.  
- Do not tour Exchange as the hero path.  
- Do not make Appraisal / Score the emotional center.  
- Do not ship platform chrome (Log in / Sign up to `#`) before those URLs are real.  
- Do not call Aseryx a marketplace, brokerage, or “desk.”

---

## Live honesty the site must keep

Until the code changes, public copy must stay consistent with:

- Listing is an explicit provider choice: appraisal proofs are created unlisted  
- Grant binds a **dataset nickname**, not the freeze hash  
- Appraisal does **not** prove fitness for the buyer’s job  
- Custody claims must be path-specific (Runtime vs Cloud vs Vault vs Access)  
- Insights are unbuilt; do not pitch them  
- Fee split in code: 80% holder / 20% platform (only if quoting economics)
