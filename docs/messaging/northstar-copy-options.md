# Northstar copy options

**Cofounder check:** current Home is not coherent enough.

**Northstar (essence, cleaned):**  
You can turn a live database into a licensable asset without giving access to the entire database.

**Product essence (what we actually do):**  
Appraise and package proprietary data on their own machine. Compliance keeps custody. They still get a quality certificate and an object they can license.

**Walk-away (two sentences):**  
You cut a dataset from the live database on your machine, get a quality certificate on it, and can license that object. The rest of the database stays put. No one gets the whole thing.

**Hard rules for every option below:** No em dashes. Prefer **database** / **dataset** / **asset**. Verification before marketplace chrome. Custody stays with them. Do not lead with “find buyers.” Certificate = quality check proof, not the sale.
